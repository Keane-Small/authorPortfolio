About the author

Maya Angelou (1928–2014) was an acclaimed American poet, memoirist, performer, and civil rights activist. She rose to international fame with her groundbreaking autobiography I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings (1969), which remains a modern classic. Through poetry collections like Phenomenal Woman and And Still I Rise, she celebrated strength, resilience, and dignity. Beyond literature, she was also a singer, dancer, actress, and educator, working with leaders such as Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and Malcolm X. Honored with over 30 honorary degrees and the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2011, her legacy endures as a voice of courage, hope, and empowerment.
